Startup sequence:
1. Push in button on the side so the device turns on.
2. Device asks if you want to connect to the network to see data on your phone
3. User accepts/declines
4. Connects to wifi/Bluetooth if accepted, fully connects to the app where it will display the data
5. Starts working, displaying measurements

During operation:
- Person can click button to the side to enter settings and click the same button again to exit.
In settings he can:
Disconnect/connect to Wifi/Bluetooth
Change language
Change fonts
Change time and date
Enble child lock feature

At any time:
- Unpush the power button to cut off power form the device and turn it off 09898
